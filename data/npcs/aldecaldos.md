## **ALDECALDOS**

### NOMAD

**Background:** The average Nomad among the Aldecaldos is nothing special, but they’re leagues better than the typical rabble they end up encountering along Night City’s edges. Most often seen in oversized bikes along the dunes of the Badlands, these riders of the storm occasionally poke deeper into Pacifica for odd jobs.

### GUNSLINGER

**Background:** The tougher fighters among the Aldecaldos tend to lead smaller bands of riders into the city for work, or just hunt down their own contracts on the side. These Gunslingers are capable fighters, sport solid armaments, and usually have cybernetic reinforcements. They’re not as strong as some corp mercs, but they can hold their own.
