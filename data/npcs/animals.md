## **ANIMALS**

### GUNNER

**Background:** The lowest of the Animals’ ranks are those only permitted the most basic of firearms to act as fodder for their more experienced compatriots. These Gunners often work alongside tougher Animals’ members that they’re training under to earn their place in the pack. They can put out some damage, but aren’t huge threats on their own.
