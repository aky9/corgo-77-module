**Background:** Lazarus Group’s post-Arasaka return to more of a PMC focus has resulted in their operators becoming some of the best “special forces for hire” in the world once more. Some claim Lazarus is much stronger than they let their competition believe, and when you face one of their soldiers in combat, you might start to believe that too.
