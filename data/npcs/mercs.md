## **MERCS**

### BLAZER

**Background:** When you start as a freelance merc, you might get called a ‘Blazer’ for a while. This is in reference to those who want to burn fast and burn high without a care for their personal well-being, and is definitely not at all a derogatory term. This doesn’t mean actual Blazers are totally inept, they just haven’t been in the game for longer than a few weeks.
