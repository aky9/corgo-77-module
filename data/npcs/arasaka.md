## **ARASAKA**

### GUARD

**Background:** The lowest bottom-feeder of Arasaka’s security details is the humble security guard. They defend corporate interests all across the globe, with gear and armor only slightly better than what you can get on the street. Still, they’re just the fodder before the real threats show up, so make sure they don’t call on their friends.

### RANGER

**Background:** Entirely different from Militech’s rangers, these are corporate semi-soldiers with smartguns that’ll shoot you if you look at them wrong. They have next to no subtlety and are provided combat cyberware to do whatever the corp asks. So yeah, not the same whatsoever — Arasaka’s use heavy SMGs instead of shotguns, after all.

### SANDAYU ODA

**Background:** Hanako Arasaka's personal bodyguard, trained by Goro Takemura, the head of security for the Arasaka family. Oda is one of the deadliest cyberninjas in the world, with more custom gear and cyberware than most mercs will know about in their entire lives. If you ever face him, your best options are to run or pray for divine intervention to smite this demonic spirit. While he is extremely deadly, his honor often causes him to face opposition entirely alone, and he is thus susceptible to a theoretical overwhelming ambush or trap. It just better be a good trap with a lot of EMPs, because Oda will gut most foes like a fish in the blink of an eye.

*“If Oda has ulterior motives, he will need no elites,*  
*no air support, no one but himself. Trust me,*  
*I have seen him work."*

- *Goro Takemura*
