## **MILITECH**

### RECON SUPPORT

**Background:** When you need grunts to get somewhere quick and hit the flanks hard, you outfit them with lighter armor and close-quarters weaponry. This tactic especially favors recruits with less natural strength or cybernetics, as they’re also the most expendable among Militech’s fodder. This is why they’re almost never sent out alone, and are more often the backup for the heavier hitters in a squad.
