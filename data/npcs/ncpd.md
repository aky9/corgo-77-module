### BEAT COP

**Background:** Beat Cops are the most basic of basic law enforcement, and typically are relegated to crime scene securement or simple odd-jobs. They aren’t heavy fighters, but are still gifted solid gear from the NCPD’s armory that helps marginally boost their chances of survival against anyone other than a homeless refugee.
