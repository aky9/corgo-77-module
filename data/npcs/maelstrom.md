## **MAELSTROM**

### SCOUT

**Background:** Scouts are the closest thing to basic slice-n-dicers that Maelstrom possess, and are fittingly skilled and equipped. All Maelstrom members sport more chrome than similar counterparts in other gangs, as part of their initiation process is having their eyes (and a limb sometimes) plucked out — all while they’re still awake.
