## **NUSA**

### BLACK OPS OPERATIVE

**Background:** You’ve never heard of them, and you never will. These operatives are sent on only the most clandestine of missions the NUSA can find, and are pulled from all over the country’s top military units. They’re ruthlessly trained and rigorously equipped, with each operative typically picking their favorite loadout for the mission at hand. Some say a full team of these operatives could kill a moon if they wanted to.
